The basics

We've tried to keep it simple.

Getting in

Use the keypad on the lower-level double doors. Your code is the last 4 digits of the phone number on your reservation; it's also in your Airbnb messages.

Getting here

Apple Maps works best. The house is toward the end of a single-lane private dirt road. Park at the top of the main (steep) driveway or in front of the garage.

Wi-Fi

timberside-guest, no password. We have surprisingly fast service.

Check-in · check-out

Check-in from 4pm. Check-out by 10am on the day your booking ends.

Questions

Message us anytime through Airbnb.

02

Around the house

How to work the heat, the tub, the music and the fire.

Heat

One ecobee thermostat controls the whole house. We like it at 66–67°, but do you.

Hot tub

  • Four clips (one on each corner) hold the cover down. Unlatch all four before you lift it.
  • For jets and lights, tap the screen, then tap 1 followed by 2.
  • Please change only the temperature and jets/lights.
  • Keep the cover closed when the tub isn't in use. No glass in or around the tub.
  • Super helpful: one capful of booster after each use. It's by the door.

Cooktop vent

Press the ⇕ button to raise the vent and start the fan. Pick a speed with the small/medium/large fan buttons. Press ⇕ again to stop and lower it.

Sonos (music in every room)

Join timberside-guest, then download and open the Sonos app to play from your streaming services.

Turntable: in the Sonos app, open the System tab and choose “Line In”. This video shows how.

TVs

The Samsung smart TVs turn on with the remote. Press Home for the apps, and sign in to your own accounts if you like (remember to sign out).

Fire pit

Wood is under the hot tub. Please cover the pit again when you're done.

Winter

Snow days, ski days and après.

The road

Our road is a single-lane private dirt road, so we recommend AWD or 4WD in winter. Park at the top of the main driveway, in front of the garage, or in the lower area near the Airstream. If it snows while you're here, the plow driver will work around your cars.

Skiing nearby

Magic Mountain · closest, about 8 min Map ↗The closest mountain. Even if you don't ski Magic, the Black Line Tavern upstairs in the base lodge has a great après scene, live music often, and good bar food.

Bromley · about 20 min Map ↗On Rt 11 past Londonderry. Stop at Johnny Seesaw's on the way back; it usually has a bonfire going in the afternoon.

Stratton · a bit over 20 min Map ↗Through Rawsonville. Honeypie is on the way for smashburgers and shakes.

Okemo · about 25 min Map ↗In Ludlow.

Snow days at the house

  • The hot tub is at its best when it's snowing. Keep the cover closed in between so it stays hot.
  • The fire pit works year-round; wood is under the hot tub.
  • The chalet sits on 10 acres with old logging roads to explore, and the land between us and Magic is part of the Vermont Land Trust.

Places to eat

You have a lot of options, but we really like these.

Black Line Tavern · 4 mi, 8 min Map ↗

Upstairs in the Magic Mountain base lodge. Great après scene, live music often, pretty good bar food.

New American Grill · 7 mi, 10 min Map ↗

Same plaza as the grocery store in Londonderry. Diverse menu, lots of seating, often live music.

SoLo Farm & Table · 8 mi, 15 min Map ↗

South Londonderry, just off Rt 100. Reservations required (solofarmandtable.com). Chef's tasting menu a few nights a week; private dining by request.

Honeypie · 11 mi, 20 min Map ↗

In Rawsonville, on the way to Stratton. Great smashburgers, fries and shakes in an old service station. Often has homemade sausages and other provisions to go.

Johnny Seesaw's · 13 mi, 20 min Map ↗

On Rt 11 just before Bromley; reservations recommended (Resy). Old-lodge feel, upscale menu, very good food. Usually a bonfire outside in the afternoon.

Gringo Jack's · 21 mi, 30 min Map ↗

On Main Street in Manchester. Good margaritas, chips and guac; burgers, sandwiches and wraps too.

Groceries & supplies

All the basics in Londonderry, about 10 minutes away.

GroceriesLondonderry Village Market Map ↗, at Rt 11 & Rt 100. Solid grocery store with a decent beer and wine selection.

MeatVermont Butcher Shop Map ↗, the red building across from the Maple Leaf Diner on Rt 11. A great local butcher.

LiquorLondonderry Liquor Store Map ↗, sharing space with Jelly's Convenience Store on North Main St.

HardwareLondonderry Hardware Map ↗, in the plaza between the Village Market and New American Grill.

Other good spots

Bromley Market Map ↗: red building between Johnny Seesaw's and Bromley. Gas, great sandwiches, good beer, and Vermont cheese and maple.

JJ Hapgood's Map ↗: general store in Peru (turn right before Johnny Seesaw's). Prepared foods and pop-up specialties.

Corner Market Deli Map ↗: on the corner in South Londonderry, on the way to Stratton. Good breakfast sandwiches and coffee.

06

Summer & fall

Adventure stuff and relaxy stuff.

Walks & hikes

  • Hapgood Pond Map ↗: a little farther, but the nicer walk.
  • Lowell Lake State Park Map ↗: picnic tables, a car-top boat launch, fishing, and an easy/moderate 3.5-mile loop around the lake.
  • The Long Trail Map ↗: a big parking area between Bromley and Manchester gives access to the Appalachian/Long Trail.
  • Putney Mountain Map ↗: 926 conserved acres and 15+ miles of trails along the Windmill Hill ridgeline.
  • Right here: 10 acres with old logging roads to explore.

Adventure

  • Bromley Mountain Adventure Park Map ↗: alpine slide, mini golf, climbing wall, bumper boats, waterslide. bromley.com
  • The Battenkill in Manchester Map ↗: canoe, kayak or tube with Vermont River Runners on 30 miles of river, fast sections to slow cobble-bottom stretches.
  • Mountain biking: lift-served at Stratton, Okemo, Killington and others. vmba.org lists more trails, like Grafton and Ascutney.

Relaxing

  • Golf at Stratton Map ↗ or Fox Run in Ludlow Map ↗, each about 25 minutes away.
  • Disc golf at Magic Mountain Map ↗: 18 holes, June 1 – Oct 31, $10 per person. magicmtn.com
  • Rock River Map ↗: a wide river bed to set up with chairs and a cooler. A bit of a hike in; the area closest to parking is family friendly (some clothing-optional spots farther down the trail).
  • Taylor Farm Map ↗: lots of animals and a nice farm store for all the Vermont goodness.

Checking out

By 10am on the day your booking ends.

Before you leave, please:

  • Put garbage in the cans in the lower level of the garage. Never leave garbage outside.
  • Take all perishables out of the fridge.
  • Load and start the dishwasher.
  • Strip the linens from used beds.
  • Turn off lights, electronics and appliances.
  • Close and lock all windows and doors.
  • Leave the house the way you found it.

A 5-star review helps us a lot. If anything about your stay was off, message us on Airbnb so we can make it right.
